ONE HEAD, THREE OUTPUTS

A switch on the head sets 15, 20 or 25 W. The part number is the 20 W setting, 2,000 lm; the same head gives 1,500 lm at 15 W or 2,450 lm at 25 W, set on site to suit the display.

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

An LED track head for retail display lighting. A CRI 95 chip-on-board source (Luminus CXM-11) behind a 15° optic puts a tight, high-colour-quality spot on product, with an 8° shielding angle. The cylinder head is 3.15 in (80 mm) across, rotates 360° on the track and tilts 90°. A switch on the head selects 15, 20 or 25 W. The driver dims on TRIAC (leading-edge) or ELV (trailing-edge) dimmers from 1 to 100 percent by constant current reduction. The adapter fits H-type 3-wire, 1-circuit track. Brushed aluminum finish (white and black also offered); honeycomb louver and snoot accessories are available.
