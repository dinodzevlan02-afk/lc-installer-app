PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

The LC OPTIMUS SERIES ultra area luminaire is an efficient, cost-effective light for exterior area projects. A rugged die-cast aluminum housing carries high-efficacy 5050 LEDs behind a polycarbonate lens, with a weather-tight driver compartment that opens without tools for easy maintenance. Three housing sizes cover 75 W to 420 W, Type III, IV and V distributions come from a field-rotatable lens, and five brackets fit tenons, arms, poles and flat surfaces. The 1-10V dimmable driver is available for 100-277 VAC or 277-480 VAC.
