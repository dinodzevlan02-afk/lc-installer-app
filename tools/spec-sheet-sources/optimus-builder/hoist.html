BUILT TO BE MOVED

Set the MD on its tripod, crank it up to 13 ft and plug it in. Need it overhead instead? The HB hangs from a ceiling hook or chain and plugs into an outlet. No hardwiring either way.

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

LC HOIST is a portable 300 W LED area light for sites where the lighting moves with the work. The light engine sits in a 15.7 in (400 mm) round, passively cooled aluminum disk, sealed to IP65 and rated IK08 against impact. It comes in two set-ups: the MD mounts on a post adapter and a hand-crank tripod for free-standing area light, and the HB hangs from its hook as a temporary high bay. Both run on 100-277 VAC and are ordered in one colour temperature from 2700K to 6500K.

SET-UP, MD ON THE TRIPOD

  • Open the legs fully on level ground and lock every wing nut.
  • Fit the disk on the post adapter at the top of the column and lock it.
  • Turn the crank clockwise to raise the column, counterclockwise to lower it; lock at height.
  • Guy the tripod with the safety cables to the ground stakes, always, outdoors or in wind.
  • Keep the load within 66.1 lb (30 kg).

SET-UP, HB

  • Screw the lifting hook firmly into the disk.
  • Hang from a hook or support rated for 34.4 lb (15.6 kg) or more and close the spring clip.
  • Fit the safety chain as a second support, then plug in.
