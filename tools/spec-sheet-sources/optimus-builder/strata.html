PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

A 4 ft T8 LED tube for relamping fluorescent troffers, wraps and strips. As a Type A tube it runs on the T8 fluorescent ballast already in the fixture, so the lamp is swapped and nothing is rewired. On a normal-ballast-factor ballast it gives 1,850 lm at 4000K and CRI 83 from 10 W of lamp power; with the ballast, the system draws 12.5 W. Light output and system power depend on the ballast the tube runs on.

OVERVIEW

  • Type A: runs on the existing T8 fluorescent ballast
  • 1,850 lm on a normal-ballast-factor T8 ballast (tolerance ±10 percent)
